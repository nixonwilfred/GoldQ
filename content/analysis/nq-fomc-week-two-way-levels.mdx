## 1H Market Structure — Bearish CHoCH

NQ tapped into the **26,520–26,540** area, sweeping the Buy Side Liquidity (BSL) resting above the prior 1H swing high at **26,500**. That sweep was immediately rejected and price printed a **Change of Character (CHoCH)** on the 1-hour — a lower high has formed relative to the recent swing, and the 1H structure has shifted bearish.

The 1H bias is short. This is not a resistance level — it is a liquidity sweep followed by a structural shift. Those are two different things, and the distinction matters for how you execute.

## 15M Confluence — MSS + FVG

The 15-minute chart has confirmed a **Market Structure Shift (MSS)**: price broke below the prior 15M swing low at **26,420**, validating bearish order flow on the intermediate timeframe.

Inside the displacement move that created the MSS, there is an open **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** between **26,370 and 26,395** — an imbalance left behind by aggressive institutional selling. Price will likely retrace into this gap before continuing lower.

The **26,430–26,460** zone is a **Breaker Block** — a former area of 15M demand that was consumed and flipped. Any retracement into this area on the 15M is further confluence for short entries.

## 15M Liquidity Sweep Trigger (Alternative Entry)

If price does not immediately retrace into the FVG, watch for a **liquidity sweep of the prior 15M low** below **26,370**. A sweep of those lows followed by a rejection and reclaim is a secondary entry trigger — the sweep clears stop orders, and the reclaim signals the continuation move is beginning.

## Entry — 1M Reaction Candle

Once price enters the **26,370–26,395 FVG** (or sweeps the 15M low), drop to the **1-minute** chart. Wait for a clear reaction candle: bearish engulfing, a wick rejection, or a 1M MSS forming within the zone. That candle is the entry.

No 1M confirmation = no entry. The FVG provides the area; the 1M candle provides the timing.

## Targets

- **Target 1 (1:1 RR):** Close 50% of the position at 1:1 from entry. Capital protection first.
- **Target 2 (Liquidity):** The Sell Side Liquidity (SSL) pool on the 1H sits at **26,180–26,220**, below the prior 1H swing low. That is the runner target.

## Invalidation

A **1H close back above 26,500** reclaims the BSL zone, negates the CHoCH, and invalidates the bearish thesis. If that happens, no trade.
