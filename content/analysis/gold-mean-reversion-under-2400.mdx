## 1H Market Structure — Bias is Bearish

Gold extended into the **4,695–4,710** area and swept the Buy Side Liquidity (BSL) resting above the prior 1H swing high at **4,688**. That sweep printed a clear **Change of Character (CHoCH)** on the 1-hour — the first lower high has formed, and structure has shifted bearish. The 1H bias is now short until price gives us a reason to reassess.

This is not a support rejection. Price engineered liquidity above a key swing and reversed — that is a structural signal, not a coincidence.

## 15M Setup — MSS Confirmed, IFVG in Range

After the 1H CHoCH, the 15-minute chart is printing a **Market Structure Shift (MSS)** — a lower low below the last 15M swing low at **4,668**, confirming the sell-side narrative is active.

There is an open **Inverse Fair Value Gap (IFVG)** between **4,660 and 4,672** from the displacement candle that broke structure. Price is likely to retrace into this IFVG before the next leg lower — this is the entry zone.

A **Breaker Block** at **4,678–4,690** (the former 15M demand that failed) adds confluence to the sell side above the IFVG.

## Entry — 1M Reaction Candle

Wait for price to retrace into the **4,660–4,672 IFVG** on the 15M. Drop to the **1-minute** chart and look for a reaction candle — a bearish engulfing, a rejection wick, or a 1M MSS forming inside that zone. That is the entry trigger.

Do not enter before the 1M confirmation. The IFVG gives the zone; the 1M candle gives the timing.
